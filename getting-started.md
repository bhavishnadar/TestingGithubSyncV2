# Getting Started {#h_d9f57e5b76}

Welcome aboard! 🎉 This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a brand-new account to a fully configured workspace in just a few minutes. No prior setup required — just follow along step by step.

## 1\. Set up your profile {#h_d767520391}

Head to **Settings → Account** and add your name, a profile photo, and your timezone. Your timezone matters more than you might think — it keeps every timestamp, report, and scheduled action aligned to your local time, so nothing shows up an hour off.

While you're here, add a short bio so teammates know who they're working with. A friendly profile makes collaboration feel a lot more personal.

## 2\. Configure your preferences {#h_d9b0f9c651}

Choose how you want to be notified:

- **Email** — daily summaries and important alerts
- **Push** — real-time updates while you're on the go
- **In-app** — everything, as it happens

You can fine-tune these at any time, so don't worry about getting it perfect on day one.

## 3\. Invite your team {#h_7a8ccde78d}

Collaboration is better together. Go to **Members**, click **Invite**, and add your teammates so everyone can jump in. Each person gets their own login and can be assigned a role that fits their responsibilities.

💡 **Tip:** Not sure where to start? Create your first project and explore — you can change any of these settings later.

That's it — your workspace is ready. Welcome to the team, and happy building!
